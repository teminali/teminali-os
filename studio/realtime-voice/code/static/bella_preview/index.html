🧪 Fitted Voices — First Listen
bella_fit_authority and bella_fit_seductive were solved numerically by fit_style_tensor.py against measured descriptors and wired into the engine as switchable profiles — but until now neither had ever been rendered to audio. A blend that wins on F0 residual can still be unpleasant to listen to, and a cost number cannot tell you that. All three renders below were made in one run, on the same lines, each at the speed its profile actually ships with.

What the numbers already say: her two references sit in genuinely different registers — 226.4 Hz in the seduction scene, 178.7 Hz in the cold-authority scene, a 47.7 Hz swing. Each fitted voice is nearly flat across both (185.0 / 188.2 and 213.7 / 213.7). A fixed blend cannot change register per scene, so no single one of these covers both halves of her. Judge them by ear on the half each one is for.
